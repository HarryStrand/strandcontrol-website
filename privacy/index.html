Privacy policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy describes how StrandControl, LLC (“StrandControl,” “we”) handles information in the Dartopia apps for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV, and on the StrandControl website. Dartopia is a local dart scoring app. It does not require an account, does not include ads or analytics, and does not send your game data to us.

Who we are

StrandControl, LLC operates the Dartopia apps and this website. The company’s name and postal address are in the Impressum. For a privacy or legal question, email legal@strandcontrol.com. For product support, use the support form.

The website

The pages at strandcontrol.com include this policy and a support form. You do not need an account to read them. The site does not use advertising or analytics tools, and it does not sell personal information.

If you use the support form, we ask for your name, email address, an optional phone number, a topic, a subject, and a message. We use that information to reply, including when you ask a privacy question. We do not use it for marketing. The form does not ask for files unless attachments are turned on. If they are, pictures and log files you choose are sent with the message.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks the form for spam. The site also limits how often one IP address can submit the form, for one minute at a time. Those checks use the IP address of the connection.

Web3Forms receives the message, emails it to us, and stores a copy. Web3Forms hosts that storage on Amazon Web Services and says it keeps form submissions for up to three years, unless they are deleted sooner. Web3Forms says it may send the email address and IP address to spam-checking services (CleanTalk and Akismet). When a message is accepted, the site tries to email a short thank-you to the address you entered, using Cloudflare Email. We keep our own copy only as long as we need it to handle your request and any follow-up, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Cloudflare serves the website. A request includes the IP address, the page address, and the language preference sent by the browser. The site uses that language preference only to choose the language of the response, and does not store the choice itself. Cloudflare keeps request logs to run and protect the site, and those logs can include the request headers.

Cloudflare and Web3Forms process this information in the United States, India, and other countries where their systems run. Web3Forms says transfers from the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are made under standard contractual clauses, as described in its data-processing terms.

What the Dartopia app does not do

The app does not:

  • Create user accounts or require a sign-in
  • Send your game data to us
  • Use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs
  • Sell personal information
  • Use data for tracking across other companies’ apps or websites

App Store payments, if you buy Unlock All Games, are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.

Information stored on your device

You can use Dartopia without entering personal details. If you choose to, the following stays on that device unless you share a game on your local network (see below).

Information Where it is stored Why
Player names On device Roster, scoreboard, past-game records
Optional player photos On device (from Camera or your photo library) Avatars on the scoreboard and summaries
Game scores, archives, postponed games, win/loss and marks-per-round stats On device Scoring, history, and player profiles
Settings (sound, Dart Bot skill, past-game limits, nearby sharing, device name) On device App preferences
Nearby device identifier and the list of devices you have allowed to connect On device Remember permitted remotes and Apple TVs
Optional guest Wi-Fi name and password On-device Keychain Show a QR code so guests can join that network

Camera access is used only if you take a profile photo. Photo library access is used only if you choose an existing photo. Local Network access is used to find and talk to other Dartopia devices you choose to connect.

Apple TV stores which sharing device it last used so it can reconnect. It does not keep a player roster of its own.

Local network sharing

If you turn on Share nearby (or join a nearby device, or use Apple TV as a scoreboard), Dartopia sends game information over your local network only to devices that connect. That can include player names, scoreboard state, and small copies of player photos.

  • New remotes and Apple TVs need permission on the hosting iPhone or iPad the first time.
  • Sharing uses the local network (and standard Apple device-to-device networking). It is not uploaded to us.
  • Anyone who can see the guest Wi-Fi QR code can read the network name and password you stored. Do not show that code to people who should not join the venue Wi-Fi.
  • Turn off Share nearby, remove an authorized device in Settings, or leave the game on Apple TV to stop sharing with that device.

How long information stays on your device

Information in the app stays on the device until you delete it or remove the app.

You can:

  • Delete a player (except Dart Bot), including their photo, from Settings
  • Remove a profile photo without deleting the player
  • Delete past games with a swipe on Home
  • Remove guest Wi-Fi in Settings
  • Remove an authorized nearby device in Settings
  • Delete Dartopia, which removes on-device data for that copy of the app (Keychain items may remain until you delete them in Settings or remove the app, depending on iOS)

We cannot delete data from your device remotely because we never receive it.

Third parties

  • Apple. App Store, In-App Purchase, iCloud backups of device data if you use iCloud Backup, and operating-system services. Apple’s privacy policy applies to those services.
  • Libraries in the app. Dartopia uses Apple frameworks and the Haversack library to store guest Wi-Fi credentials in the Keychain on your device. Those libraries do not send Dartopia data to us.
  • Cloudflare. Hosting for this website, the Turnstile spam check on the support form, request logs, and the thank-you email.
  • Web3Forms. Delivery and storage of support messages. Web3Forms names Amazon Web Services for that storage, and CleanTalk and Akismet for spam checks.

If you buy Unlock All Games, Apple processes the purchase under Apple’s terms. Extra games unlock on that Apple ID’s devices through Apple’s purchase system once that purchase is enabled.

Children

Dartopia is not directed at children and is not in the Kids Category. Do not add photos of children unless you are authorized to do so. This website is not directed at children. If you believe a child sent us a support message, email legal@strandcontrol.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and requests

For information that stays on your device, the controls above are how you access, correct, or delete it. We cannot do that from our side, because the app does not send that information to us.

For information we hold from the website, you can ask us to access it, correct it, delete it, limit or stop processing, or provide a copy of what you sent. You can also complain to a data protection authority where you live. We will respond as applicable law requires.

Email legal@strandcontrol.com, or use the support form and choose “Privacy question or concern.”

You can also withdraw camera, photo, or local network permission in iOS or tvOS Settings. Some features will not work without those permissions.

Apple TV

The Apple TV app is a scoreboard. It receives game state from a nearby iPhone or iPad running Dartopia. It does not score, does not offer In-App Purchase, and does not send that game state to us.
